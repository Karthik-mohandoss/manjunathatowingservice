Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Manjunatha Towing Service ("we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us when seeking car towing and car recovery assistance.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, call our phone number (98453 84726), contact us via WhatsApp (+91 98453 84726), or submit our quick enquiry form, we may collect:

  • Contact details: Your name and mobile phone number.
  • Location details: Your breakdown pickup spot, nearest landmark, and destination garage.
  • Vehicle details: Your passenger car make, model, and the nature of the breakdown.
  • Technical usage data: Non-personally identifiable analytics data such as browser type, referring page, and conversion interactions to optimize website performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for legitimate service purposes:

  • To evaluate driver and tow truck availability for your vehicle.
  • To contact you by phone or WhatsApp to quote towing charges and provide an estimated arrival time.
  • To safely dispatch our recovery tow truck to your vehicle's location.
  • To maintain accurate operational records.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We strictly value your personal privacy:

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal phone numbers or breakdown details to third-party telemarketers or advertisers.
  • We do not push personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name or phone number into website analytics platforms or Google advertising tags.
  • Information shared over WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption and privacy practices.

4. Third-Party Services & Google Advertising

We may utilize privacy-compliant web analytics and advertising measurement tools (such as Google Ads and Google Analytics) to assess advertising effectiveness. These tools use cookies or pseudonymous identifiers to measure anonymous link clicks and page engagements. You can control or disable cookies via your browser settings.
